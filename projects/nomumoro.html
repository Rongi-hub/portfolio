Primary logo

A primary logo is your brand’s main, most detailed visual signature. It should be used most of the time whenever you have plenty of space and a wide canvas.

Logomark

A logomark is the standalone symbol, icon, or graphic element of your logo. It should be used when your full business name would be too tiny to read, or when your brand is already well-known in that context.

Logo clear space

Logo clear space exists as an invisible buffer that keeps the logo legible, uncluttered, and visually distinct on any layout.
