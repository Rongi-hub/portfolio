Physte Mobile Application

Client
Tampere university of applied sciences
Deliverables
Figma prototype
Role
UX designer

Overview

Physte is a mobile application that provides detailed instructions on stretching exercises and effective workouts. Findings from user research and user interviews helped shaping the app. The application includes animated video instructions and mascots with matching voice interactions to effectively appeal to the target audience.

Research & discovery

User research and interviews revealed how the target audience engages with fitness content on mobile. These insights informed the information architecture, visual tone, and interaction patterns — prioritising clarity of instruction and motivational elements that resonate with users.

Design highlights

  1. Animated video instructions

Step-by-step visual guidance for stretching and workout routines, designed for easy follow-along during exercise.

  1. Mascot-driven voice interactions

Character mascots paired with voice feedback to create an engaging, approachable experience tailored to the target audience.
