Build a Mothership of Multiple Design Systems

Client
Hiab Finland Oy
Deliverables
Figma file, Components, Color tokens
Role
UX designer

Overview

Hiab was in need of a primary foundation for multiple design systems developed across various projects. I helped Hiab create a centralised design system including accessibility guidelines, visual guidelines, and adjustable components.

Also, the new design system introduced color tokens with Variables, which help tremendously unite color system between design and implementation. The design system became a scalable document that different teams could branch from, customise, and maintain consistently across various products.

Key deliverables

  1. Centralised Figma library with reusable components

A shared component set that teams could adopt and extend without rebuilding foundations from scratch.

  1. Color tokens using Figma Variables

Token-based color system bridging design and development, reducing inconsistencies during handoff.

  1. Accessibility and visual guidelines

Documented principles ensuring products across Hiab's portfolio meet consistent quality and accessibility standards.

Impact

The mothership design system enabled multiple product teams to work from a shared foundation while maintaining flexibility for product-specific needs. This reduced duplication, improved consistency, and accelerated design-to-development collaboration across Hiab's digital products.
