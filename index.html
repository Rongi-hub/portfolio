I’m a product designer with a background in furniture and spatial design. I love finding human-centred solutions to make improvements in design, system and life.

  1. 1.

    Design for the real situation

    I start from where people will be and what they’ll be dealing with when they use the product, and build accessibility in from the start.

  2. 2.

    Make decisions backed by rationales

    I look for proof instead of relying on taste alone to make decisions. Interviews, usability tests and data help me find what really works for people.

  3. 3.

    Build systems, not just screens

    A single screen solves one problem, but a system solves the next hundred. I create design foundations that teams can share, adapt and maintain, which saves time and keeps every product feeling like one.
